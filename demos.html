Build 8 · Tester and reviewer walkthroughs

Make something yours.

Create a garden invitation, refine its words with on-device AI, choose typography, add a real image with its credit, then share it.

These are real simulator recordings inside illustrative device frames. Long pauses and portions of typing have been cut. The app screens and AI results are real. There is no audio; step captions and the written walkthrough below describe the actions. These clips do not replace testing on physical devices.

Follow along

  1. In Create, choose Invitation. Replace the project name, headline and details with your own.
  2. Tap the sparkles button. Choose Suggest improvements, compare the current and proposed wording, edit a suggestion if you like, then Apply. Undo is available until your next edit.
  3. In Style, choose a headline font, alignment and size. You can also change every palette color or switch the visual style.
  4. Choose Find images online. Search for a subject, open a result and inspect its source, license and credit before adding it. Saved images can be used offline.
  5. Open Share to preview your design and send a PNG or PDF using the native share sheet. Image credits remain in the export.

The demo uses a public-domain Vincent van Gogh sunflower painting from The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Check each image’s original source and rights before publishing your own work.

AI needs an Apple Intelligence-compatible device with a supported language, Apple Intelligence enabled and its model downloaded. Manual editing works without AI. Image search requires internet access and sends search terms and request information to the selected collections and image hosts. Individual collections may be unavailable.

TestFlight testing is free. The planned public app is a one-time paid download with no subscription or in-app purchases.
