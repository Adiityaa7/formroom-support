Piclilt · Build 10

Make something yours.

Place a sticker on a video. Turn a description into an editable invitation. See the actual app in these short walkthroughs.

Real simulator recordings inside illustrative device frames. Pauses and parts of typing are shortened. The app screens and AI results are real. These silent clips have step captions; the written steps below describe the actions. They do not demonstrate physical-device cutout or Apple Image Playground generation.

Try it yourself

  1. In Create, tap Video and select a clip up to one minute. Add a sticker from Photos or web search, adjust its position, size and rotation, then play and share. Original audio is retained in exported MP4.
  2. Or describe an invitation, post or flyer. Include real dates, names and details. Create with AI, or choose a manual template when AI is unavailable.
  3. Tap text on the design to edit it. Use Look for fonts and colors, Arrange for sections, and Stickers for photo/cutout options.
  4. Ask AI for a revision, review the preview and Apply. Undo and Redo remain available.
  5. Share PNG/PDF or an editable backup. Keep any image credits when publishing.

Text AI requires compatible Apple Intelligence hardware, a supported language/region, and an enabled, downloaded model. Manual tools work without AI. Cutout success varies by image. Apple's emoji-style generation needs a supported physical device and may alter or decline people or characters; it has not been verified in these simulator demos.

Web discovery requires internet and sends short search words and request information to the collections and image hosts. Apple image creation may use Private Cloud Compute. Photos and videos are processed locally unless you explicitly choose image creation or sharing.

TestFlight is free. The planned public app costs US $4.99 once, with regional pricing, no subscription and no in-app purchases.
