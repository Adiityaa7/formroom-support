Describe it.
Make it yours.

Create an invitation, post, flyer or announcement from a description. Edit the design itself, add images and personal stickers, then share it.

From an idea to a design

  1. In Create, describe what you want. Add the real date, time and details. Choose Create with AI on a supported device, or start with a template.
  2. Tap words on the canvas to change them. Hold and drag sections to reorder. Use Look for colors and style, or Ask AI to request a revision. Review before applying; Undo and Redo are available.
  3. Add a photo or sticker, check the finished design and use the top Share button for PNG, PDF, text or an editable backup.

Projects save on this device. No account, ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. TestFlight is free. The planned US App Store price is $4.99 once; the App Store displays your local price. Beta access does not include a later paid App Store purchase.

The steps below use build 12. In TestFlight, check the build number and update when it is available to your group. Earlier builds have different sticker options.

Use Apple’s Memoji avatars

  1. Open Create → Stickers → Memoji & emoji from keyboard.
  2. Tap the input box, switch to the emoji keyboard, then open Memoji. Choose an expression. To create or edit your avatar, use + or More (…) in that panel.
  3. Review the preview, then tap Use sticker. Tap the sticker on your creation to resize, rotate or share it as a transparent PNG. Drag to move it.

Memoji is customized by hand; typing a celebrity name or choosing a photo does not automatically create a matching Memoji. Apple’s keyboard controls available stickers and avatar editing. If Memoji is missing, create one in Messages → Stickers → Memoji, or copy a sent sticker and use Paste in Piclilt.

Choose Video → Stickers for the same picker on a clip. Imported Memoji and emoji are still images, not animated avatars. Genmoji creation is available only when offered by Apple Intelligence on your device.

Apple’s keyboard and Memoji instructions

Make a cartoon or cutout on your iPhone

  1. Open Create → Stickers. Search a subject such as Jungkook, or choose Make a sticker from Photos. For one person's avatar, use a clear solo portrait rather than a group photo.
  2. Tap a reference photo. Choose Remove background for a transparent photo cutout. Review the edges, then tap Use cutout sticker.
  3. For a drawn character, choose Cartoon avatar. Select Smile, Wink, Love, Wow, Laugh or Cool. Change hair, skin tone, hair color, shirt, earrings and face width. Tap Use cartoon sticker. This local drawing can match photo colors; it is not an exact likeness or AI-generated reconstruction.
  4. Drag the sticker on your design. Tap it for size and rotation, Edit cartoon & expression, or Share sticker PNG. The PNG keeps transparency. Share it to Files or an app that accepts images or custom stickers.

For Apple-generated cartoon and emoji options

From the reference screen, choose AI cartoon & emoji, pick Emoji or Cartoon and an expression, then Generate options. Choose a result in Apple's screen and tap Done. Back in Piclilt, preview it and tap Use AI sticker; Remove its background is available for an opaque result.

Apple controls availability and accepted subjects, and may decline public figures or a particular reference. Apple Intelligence must be enabled, downloaded and available for the device, language and region. Apple image generation may use Private Cloud Compute. The local Cartoon avatar option works without Apple Intelligence. No official celebrity, anime or Bitmoji pack is bundled.

Cutouts work best with a distinct subject. You can use the original under Use the original photo instead. The app exports sticker images; it does not install a system keyboard or messaging extension. Retain required source credits.

If the app closes unexpectedly

Update in TestFlight, retry the same search and select the same reference. Use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight and include your iPhone model, iOS version, build number and the last button tapped. If iOS offers to share a crash report when reopening, sending it helps identify the cause. Keep a backup of your projects; do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step.

A few useful answers

What works offline?

Manual editing, cartoon avatars, saved images, cutouts, previews, the reference catalog and exports work locally. Apple’s text AI runs on-device after its model is downloaded. New web searches and image downloads need internet access. Apple Image Playground may use Apple Private Cloud Compute on newer systems and should not be assumed to work offline. Sharing destinations and external links have their own requirements.

What does the AI need?

Text AI requires iOS/iPadOS 26 or later, compatible Apple Intelligence hardware, a supported language/region, Apple Intelligence enabled and its model ready. The app shows its availability. If offered, Use English requests a supported English model language; it cannot bypass device or regional restrictions. Manual editing works on iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. Image Playground availability is separate.

Can AI automatically find matching images?

Turn on Find online images and cutouts before creating. AI chooses short search words and sends those words to Wikimedia Commons, Openverse and The Met; your full description stays on-device. Inspect the offered image and its source/license before choosing. Each provider and image host receives normal request information, including your IP address. Leave the switch off to create without web discovery.

Collections can time out or return no suitable images. Provider metadata can be incomplete. Hide an image or creator or report an issue from web image details. Reuse licenses do not cover every possible right, including endorsement or likeness rights. Inspect the original source before publishing.

How do I change text, fonts and layout?

Tap a text block to edit its wording. Its editor also offers headline font, size and alignment controls. Use Look to change the palette or visual style. Hold and drop sections to reorder, or use Arrange controls to move and hide them. Stickers can move freely within the design. Undo and Redo retain recent edits while the editor is open.

Where are design briefs and the reference library?

Open More tools in Create. App and website briefs retain the guided subject inventory, design-reference library, review rounds and prompt export. These tools prepare specifications for a designer or coding agent; they do not execute web layouts or generate app code.

How do I back up or reuse a project?

Touch and hold a project in Projects to duplicate or favorite it. In Settings, Export all projects writes an editable JSON backup. Import from Projects restores new copies, including saved images, credits and sticker placements. There is no automatic cloud sync. Back up important work before deleting the app.

How do I get help?

Replay the quick tour with the ? button on Create. During testing, use TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback and include your device, OS, app build and the steps that failed. Share screenshots only if you are comfortable with their contents.

Put stickers on a video

In Create, choose Edit a video and select a clip up to one minute (200 MB source limit). Add photos, cutouts or available Apple emoji-style results as stickers. Drag them, change their size or rotation, preview and export MP4 with the original audio. Stickers stay in place throughout the clip; this is not motion tracking or AI video generation. Processing stays on your device. Editable backups include the clip, within the backup size limit.

For a direct subject search, choose Make stickers. For example, search Jungkook; the spelling jungkok is corrected. Available options depend on reusable source images and device support. Apple may alter or decline specific likenesses.
