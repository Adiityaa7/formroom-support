Your words.
A little more considered.

Make an invitation, share a thought, announce something new, or plan your next app. Formroom gives your ideas a place to take shape, right on your iPhone.

From an idea to something to share

  1. Choose an invitation, social post, flyer or announcement in Create.
  2. Replace the example words. Choose a style and make the colors your own.
  3. Check your details, then open Share to send a PNG, PDF or plain text.

Your projects save on this iPhone. There are no accounts, ads or subscriptions. The planned App Store model is a one-time purchase; the final price will appear on the App Store.

A few useful answers

Does Formroom need the internet?

Creating, editing, previews, contrast checks and exports work offline. Opening external links and sending something through another app may use the internet.

How does the optional AI work?

Writing suggestions use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. They need iOS 26 or later, a compatible iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled, a supported language and a downloaded model. You review suggestions before applying them. All manual tools work on iOS 17 or later without AI.

How do I back up my work?

Open Settings and choose Export all projects. Save the JSON file somewhere you control. To restore it, open Projects and tap the import button. Imported projects become new copies. Formroom has no automatic cloud sync.

Can I reuse a design?

Touch and hold a project in Projects and choose Duplicate. Mark useful projects as favorites, then turn on Favorites only to find them quickly.

Why is sharing an image unavailable?

Give your project a name and headline. Graphic exports support a name up to 80 characters, headline up to 120, message up to 500, details up to 400 and footer up to 80. Longer words stay saved and can still be shared in a text brief. The canvas grows to fit your text.

Does it generate apps or code?

No. The design-brief tools prepare a specification and prompt for a designer or coding agent. PNG and PDF exports are finished static graphics.

How do I report a problem during testing?

Use TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback, or email the support address below. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that caused the problem. Send a screenshot only if you are comfortable sharing its contents.
