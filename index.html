From an idea to something to share

  1. Choose an invitation, social post, flyer or announcement in Create.
  2. Replace the example words. Choose a style, font, size and alignment, and make the colors your own.
  3. Check your details, then open Share to send a PNG, PDF or plain text.

A few useful answers

Does Offline Intelligence need the internet?

Creating, editing, previews, contrast checks, the design-reference library and exports work offline. In build 8 and later, optional image search and new downloads require internet access. Search terms and request information go to the selected collections and image hosts; saved images work offline. Opening external links and sending something through another app may use the internet.

How does the optional AI work?

Writing suggestions use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. They need iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, a compatible iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence enabled, a supported language and a downloaded model. Tap the sparkles button in the editor. Review and edit suggestions before applying them, then undo from the save bar if needed. All manual tools work on iOS or iPadOS 17 or later without AI.

How do I back up my work?

Open Settings and choose Export all projects. Save the JSON file somewhere you control. To restore it, open Projects and tap the import button. Imported projects become new copies. Offline Intelligence has no automatic cloud sync.

How do I use image search and the design library?

These features arrive in build 8. In Style, tap Find images online and search Wikimedia Commons, Openverse or The Met. Inspect the original page and license before adding an image. The app retains credits with exports and editable backups. Keep up to six references and choose one for the canvas. If a collection is unavailable, choose another; API availability is not guaranteed. This app uses Openverse but is not endorsed or certified by Openverse or the collections.

Words → Design guide helps you collect real details, define an idea and plan evidence. Explore the offline library for art directions, palettes, typography, app patterns and layout/motion references. Add references to your exported brief or apply a palette. The native editor does not execute web layouts or generate code.

Image sources can contain unsuitable or incorrectly labeled content. The app filters available labels, and you can hide an image or creator or report an issue from its details. Check licenses and other rights before publishing; CC BY-SA adaptations may need the same license.

Can I reuse a design?

Touch and hold a project in Projects and choose Duplicate. Mark useful projects as favorites, then turn on Favorites only to find them quickly.

Why is sharing an image unavailable?

Give your project a name and headline. Graphic exports support a name up to 80 characters, headline up to 120, message up to 500, details up to 400 and footer up to 80. Longer words stay saved and can still be shared in a text brief. The canvas grows to fit your text.

Does it generate apps or code?

No. The design-brief tools prepare a specification and prompt for a designer or coding agent. PNG and PDF exports are finished static graphics.

How do I report a problem during testing?

Use TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback, or email the support address below. Include your device model, OS version, app version and the steps that caused the problem. Send a screenshot only if you are comfortable sharing its contents.
