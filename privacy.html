Privacy, in plain words.

Piclilt is developed by Aditya Patil. The app keeps your work on your device and does not collect data for the developer.

What the app collects

Piclilt does not collect or transmit your projects, identifiers, usage data or analytics to the developer. It has no user accounts, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Optional online images

The app offers optional searches of Wikimedia Commons, Openverse and The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Searching sends the words you submit to the collections you select. In build 10, enabling Find online images and cutouts also authorizes an automatic search using short words selected by on-device AI when you create a design. The switch is off by default. The app discloses this before you create. Normal HTTPS requests expose your IP address, app user-agent and request information to those services. Loading previews or saving an image also contacts the image host identified by the collection, which may be a separate organization.

These services may retain search and technical request logs under their own policies, for service operation, security and analytics. Requests are not anonymized before reaching them. The developer does not receive your searches, operate a proxy or maintain a search history. Do not put private information in image search terms. Your full project, design brief and on-device AI instructions are not uploaded.

Search uses an ephemeral network session with no persistent cookie store or URL cache. Saved images and their source/creator/license metadata stay in the app’s private storage. Image/creator hiding settings are also stored locally. Search results are temporary; selecting an image saves a resized, recompressed image without the original image metadata. Photo imports and cutouts can retain transparency as PNG files. Selected images, credits and image descriptions are included in editable backups.

You can use the editor, reference catalog, saved images, previews and exports without searching online. A new image search or download needs internet access. Learn about Wikimedia’s privacy policy, Openverse’s service terms, and The Met’s privacy policy. An original image’s hosting provider has its own practices.

Your projects and backups

Projects are stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone or iPad. The operating system may include them in device backups according to your settings. Piclilt does not operate a cloud-sync service.

You can export editable JSON backups from Settings. Delete projects in the Projects tab to remove them from your active library. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Exported copies and operating-system backups must be managed separately. If the app detects an unreadable library, it preserves the original for recovery rather than replacing it automatically.

Optional Apple Intelligence

When you request writing or image-search suggestions, excerpts of the current project are processed locally using Apple’s SystemLanguageModel. Piclilt does not send these excerpts to a server, a third-party AI provider or Private Cloud Compute. Apple controls model downloads, language support and availability. Create with AI makes a new local project. Revisions to an existing project require Apply. Search suggestions stay local unless you submit a search or enable automatic image discovery before creating.

Personal photos, cutouts and Apple image creation in build 10

The system Photos picker gives the app only the images or video clips you choose. It does not grant access to your entire photo library. Selected images are resized and stripped of original metadata, then stored privately with the project if you add them. Apple Vision attempts foreground cutouts on-device.

Explore emoji versions explicitly opens Apple Image Playground with your chosen reference image and a short description. Apple controls generation, availability and safeguards. Newer operating systems may process the request through Apple Private Cloud Compute; this feature is not guaranteed to run offline. Only Apple styles are offered; the app does not enable an external-provider style. See Apple Intelligence privacy information. Apple says Private Cloud Compute request data is not stored or accessible to Apple. A selected generated result is saved locally; the developer does not receive it.

Video clips

Selected clips are prepared locally as MP4, with original capture metadata removed, then saved in private app storage. Video exports combine the clip and selected stickers on-device and retain original audio. Clips are not sent to image collections or AI models. iCloud Photos may need internet to retrieve a clip you select. Editable backups can include clip bytes within the backup size limit. Sharing passes the finished video to your chosen destination.

Sharing, exports and links

Copying a prompt places text on the system clipboard. Sharing an image, video, PDF, text or project backup passes only your selected content to the app or destination you choose. Its privacy practices apply. Temporary export files are removed when you dismiss the share sheet. Individual sticker PNG exports carry source/credit metadata and a credit caption in the share sheet; a receiving app may discard that information. Keep required credits with published images. External links open outside Piclilt and may use the internet.

Permissions

The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location or broad photo-library access. Build 10 uses the system Photos picker for images and video clips you select. Files access is initiated by your import or export action. There is no account to delete and no developer-held project data to request.

Support and beta feedback

The image-report link opens an email draft containing the image source link; you choose whether to send it. If you email support, your email address and the information you send are used to respond and resolve the issue. Do not include sensitive content unless necessary. You can request deletion of support correspondence. TestFlight feedback and diagnostics are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policies and the settings you choose; they are separate from the app’s own data practices.

This website

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, cookies added by Piclilt, contact forms or third-party embeds. GitHub may process standard technical request information to operate the hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
