Privacy, in plain words.

Offline Intelligence is developed by Aditya Patil. The app keeps your work on your device and does not collect data for the developer.

What the app collects

Offline Intelligence does not collect or transmit your projects, identifiers, usage data or analytics to the developer. It has no user accounts, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Your projects and backups

Projects are stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone or iPad. The operating system may include them in device backups according to your settings. Offline Intelligence does not operate a cloud-sync service.

You can export editable JSON backups from Settings. Delete projects in the Projects tab to remove them from your active library. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Exported copies and operating-system backups must be managed separately. If the app detects an unreadable library, it preserves the original for recovery rather than replacing it automatically.

Optional Apple Intelligence

When you request writing suggestions, excerpts of the current project are processed locally using Apple’s SystemLanguageModel. Offline Intelligence does not send these excerpts to a server, a third-party AI provider or Private Cloud Compute. Apple controls model downloads, language support and availability. Suggestions only change your project when you tap Apply.

Sharing, exports and links

Copying a prompt places text on the system clipboard. Sharing an image, PDF, text or project backup passes only your selected content to the app or destination you choose. Its privacy practices apply. Temporary graphic-export files are removed when you dismiss the share sheet. External links open outside Offline Intelligence and may use the internet.

Permissions

The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location or photo-library access. Files access is initiated by your import or export action. There is no account to delete and no developer-held project data to request.

Support and beta feedback

If you email support, your email address and the information you send are used to respond and resolve the issue. Do not include sensitive content unless necessary. You can request deletion of support correspondence. TestFlight feedback and diagnostics are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policies and the settings you choose; they are separate from the app’s own data practices.

This website

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, cookies added by Offline Intelligence, contact forms or third-party embeds. GitHub may process standard technical request information to operate the hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. For questions or support-correspondence deletion requests, email adityapatil206999@gmail.com.
